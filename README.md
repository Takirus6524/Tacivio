# Tacivio

**Tagline:**  
Share your thoughts. Anonymously.

---

## What is Tacivio?

Tacivio is a simple, cross-platform app that lets users post anonymous thoughts, confessions, or messages and see what others are sharing. The app is designed to create a safe, light-hearted space where people can express themselves freely without the pressure of profiles or personal identifiers.  

It is **not a social media platform**, a management tool, or a productivity app. It’s a place for users to share ideas, vent, or just connect through anonymous content.

## Core Features (v0.1.0)

- **Sign up / Sign in:** Users can create accounts to post anonymously.
- **Create Posts:** Post short texts anonymously.
- **Feed:** Browse posts from other users in chronological order.
- **React to Posts:** Like or react to posts to engage.
- **Delete Your Posts:** Users can remove their own posts.
- **Report Content:** Users can report offensive posts for moderation.
- **Clean UI:** Simple and intuitive interface for easy browsing and posting.

## Why Tacivio?

Tacivio is designed for users who want a **safe, anonymous space** to share thoughts and read what others are feeling — without the complexity of social networks or productivity tools. It's about **expression, connection, and simplicity**.
